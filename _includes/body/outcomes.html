What changes

Move from “it failed” to a defensible next decision.

Hermod brings the relevant evidence into one case so the team can see which explanations remain credible and what information would actually change the conclusion.

Clarify

Separate likely product behavior from test or recordkeeping artifacts.

Give engineers a shared account of what the existing evidence does and does not support.

Prioritize

Choose the smallest next check that can resolve the decision.

Avoid broad retest campaigns when a focused comparison can eliminate the leading uncertainty.

Document

Preserve why the team held, retested, redesigned, or released.

Create a reusable case record instead of rebuilding the investigation from scattered files.
