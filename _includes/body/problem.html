The business problem

Detecting a failure is not the same as explaining it.

Intermittent wireless failures often leave several plausible causes. When the available records do not clearly distinguish them, teams repeat tests, escalate to senior engineers, change designs defensively, or delay release while the real cause remains uncertain.

Time

Repeated tests consume schedules without guaranteeing an answer.

Engineering

Specialists are pulled into evidence reconstruction instead of product work.

Cost

Teams replace parts or revise firmware before the cause is established.

Risk

An unresolved issue can become a launch delay, field failure, or customer dispute.
