Sources and scope

Elena, the restaurant, employees, availability, qualifications, flat four-hour fees and roster are fictional. The dedicated worked example identifies the model, source, retained native Decision Lab runs and verification. The screenshots show its generated custom reports, separate from the Marketplace workflows.

The Complete a Commitment app definitions assess four supplied complete repairs for the remaining recorded Friday–Sunday services. They do not generate all possible rosters. A preferred repair is preferred within that supplied comparison. Earlier work contributes recorded minute totals and a last-shift boundary; the example does not retrospectively audit that work or calculate its payroll.

Each service has two cook positions and one server position for four hours. This fixture has no in-shift breaks. A required break or another operating commitment must be represented in the working intervals and covered by a supplied repair. Ten hours of rest and a forty-hour weekly limit are declared example policies; the calculation is not an employment-law compliance assessment. Labor premiums, demand forecasting, workload intensity and staff agreement need their own records and rules.

Additional expense is measured against $656 of retained remaining payroll after Nora’s unpaid cancelled shift is removed. Changed assignments are published positions assigned to a different person, including an unfilled position; they are not a count of employee notifications or a measure of fairness. The remaining-payroll limit is $856.

Google’s Workforce Scheduling documentation supports the distinction between generating shifts and assigning employees to predefined shifts. It supplies technical context, not evidence of this fictional restaurant’s results. Solver status definitions explain why a feasible result, an established optimum, proven infeasibility and an unresolved search mean different things. Staff confirmation, authorization, external publication and actual service remain outside this example’s calculation.
