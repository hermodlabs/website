How to start

Begin with one archived failure.

No instrument replacement or production integration is required for the first case. Use a failure your team has already investigated—or is still unable to explain.

01

Provide the case materials.

Share the relevant captures, logs, telemetry, test notes, and known-good comparisons.

02

Reconstruct the decision.

Identify the competing explanations, the evidence supporting each one, and the gaps preventing a defensible conclusion.

03

Choose the next useful action.

Receive a clear account of what is supported, what remains uncertain, and which focused check is most likely to settle the case.
