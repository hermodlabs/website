Set up Alex’s five-staff situation
  1. Start a new analysis and select Forms → Operating requirements → People and scope.
  2. Keep Qualified staff on the roster at 7 and Required qualified staff inside at 4. Change Unavailable qualified staff from 0 to 2, then choose Apply inputs.
  3. Choose Calculate above the input panel. The Decision summary puts all four deployments’ operating fit, additional expense and conditional guest targets in one comparison.

This produces the first article’s situation: holding and sending one promoter meet the recorded requirements. The one-person plan costs $200; at $40 contribution per genuinely additional guest, five cover its expense and six exceed it. The third-callout sequel changes only the unavailable count to three and recalculates.
