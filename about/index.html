The company and the work

One foundation.
Different decisions.

Our work begins with a practical question: how can someone inspect a plan well enough to understand it, compare it with an alternative, and revise it when the situation changes?

Hermod’s strategy engineering platform provides a shared foundation for making relationships explicit. Each application gives that foundation a vocabulary, a model, and a workflow suited to the people using it.

Hermod.gg applies it to League of Legends strategy. Change Review Lab applies it to security change review. Both are created by Hermod Labs and built on the same platform.

How we approach the work

Reasoning people can
inspect and revise.

01

Make assumptions inspectable

Expose the conditions and relationships behind a finding so a person can question the reasoning and recognize what it depends on.

Show the support behind a conclusion.

02

Preserve human judgment

Explain options and their consequences so people can bring their own knowledge, priorities, and context to a decision.

Make room for an informed choice.

03

Keep conclusions revisable

Connect conclusions to their assumptions and evidence, making it possible to revisit the reasoning when something changes.

Keep a plan connected to its situation.

Our current focus

A broad foundation.
A focused next step.

Our current commercial development focuses on security change review, beginning with CMMC readiness examples in Change Review Lab. That gives us a specific setting in which to examine requirements, supporting evidence, and the work that a change brings back into question.

Hermod.gg remains a visible application of the same foundation, serving people who want to understand and discuss team strategy. Each tool has its own audience and a direct route into the work.
