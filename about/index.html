About / Hermod Labs

Complex decisions.
Clearer coordination.

Hermod Labs is building Decision Lab, a decision engineering platform that helps people and AI systems evaluate choices, coordinate work, and manage change.

What we’re building

Bring the relationships
into view.

Complex decisions involve connected requirements, competing goals, and information scattered across documents, systems, and people.

Decision Lab brings those relationships into a structured model so users can compare workable options, understand downstream effects, and identify what evidence, approvals, or actions are needed.

Applications

People, AI agents,
or both.

The goal is to make complex decisions and changes easier to coordinate, test, and explain—whether the work involves people, AI agents, or both.

Business planning
and process design

Compare workable options against connected requirements and competing goals. Understand what a plan depends on and what needs to happen next.

Change management

Assess what a policy or technology change will affect, including the evidence, approvals, and actions needed to carry it through.

Agentic AI workflows

Coordinate specialized AI agents, or structure training examples and evaluation criteria for fine-tuning models toward a shared objective.
