Hermod Labs is the company behind Hermod’s decision engineering platform, Hermod.gg, and Review Lab. Our tools help people examine the objectives, evidence, constraints, alternatives, and tradeoffs behind a choice.

The company and the work

One foundation.
Different decisions.

Our work begins with a practical question: What counts as an improvement, and for whom? A supportable decision makes its criteria explicit enough for people to examine and challenge them.

Hermod’s decision engineering platform provides a shared foundation for that work, from everyday operational and technical choices to strategic decisions. Each application gives it a vocabulary, a model, and a workflow suited to its users.

Hermod.gg applies it to League of Legends strategy. Review Lab begins with CMMC certification as its first use case. Both are created by Hermod Labs and built on the same platform.

How we approach the work

Reasoning people can
inspect and revise.

01

Make the criteria inspectable

State the objectives, whose outcomes matter, and the evidence behind a comparison. Give people enough context to question what “better” means.

Show the basis for a judgment.

02

Preserve human judgment

Explain options, constraints, and tradeoffs so people can bring their own knowledge and priorities to a decision, including disagreement about its criteria.

Make room for an informed choice.

03

Keep conclusions revisable

Connect conclusions to their assumptions and evidence, making it possible to revisit the reasoning when something changes.

Keep a decision connected to its situation.
