In Article 5, the manager chose Tomas to review C for $150, preserving Priya's meeting at 11. Maya and Eli will review A and B. The arrangement fits the requirements; carrying it out creates commitments.

Tomas's qualifications, independence, permission to read C, and availability are confirmed. The choice still needs an accepted assignment, a usable draft, and a completed review. A calendar slot establishes when work can happen.

Suppose the manager appoints a coordinator, and the people involved accept these responsibilities. Each commitment has an owner, a time when it must be fulfilled, and evidence of completion:

Owners, commitments, timing, and evidence of completion for the selected arrangement
OwnerCommitment and timingEvidence of completion
CoordinatorBefore work begins: confirm Tomas's assignment and notify Priya.Accepted review scope, time, and $150 fee; updated assignments.
C's writerBy 11: deliver the agreed draft of C.Identified draft version Tomas confirms he can open.
TomasBy noon: complete C's independent review.Review record identifying the draft and findings.
CoordinatorAfter reviews finish: check all three met the noon deadline.Review records and completion times checked.

The manager also owns the $150 expense and arranges payment under the agreed terms. These obligations depend on the chosen arrangement. Choosing Priya would require moving her meeting. Choosing Tomas requires the external assignment and its fee.

The handoff at 11 matters. Tomas needs the agreed draft in a form he can open when his review begins. The map connects that input to the review and its expected record. The schedule places those events in time. Any extra handoff time must fit before 11 or enter a revised schedule.

Keep the commitment's status alongside its owner and deadline. Before work starts, Tomas's review can be accepted and pending. When he begins, it is in progress. Completion requires a review record tied to the actual draft. Sending an assignment, acknowledging it, and finishing it are distinct events.

A completed review may identify problems. Our current requirement is that every report receives an independent review by noon. If corrections, another review, or delivery to a customer are also required, those actions need their own owners and time. The record must say what was completed.

For your own arrangement, record who accepted each action, when it is due, and what will establish completion. Keep pending commitments visible until the evidence arrives.
