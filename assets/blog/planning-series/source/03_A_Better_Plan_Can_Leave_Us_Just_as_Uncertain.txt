Which uncertainty matters to the goal?
For a simple extension, let S = (O, L, T ) combine an additional order change before a specified release
review, availability of a loading window, and quoted transit versus one day longer. Use three independent
fair binary variables, giving eight equally likely states and H(S |e) = 3 bits. These are stipulated
probabilities.
Let Fa(s) indicate whether action a achieves a stated operating target in state s. If probabilities are
justified, its modeled target probability is

misses the target also has zero target-outcome entropy. Minimizing entropy alone therefore cannot be the
goal.
If actions change the state distribution, that effect must be modeled separately; the preceding formula
treats S as unaffected by the selected action. Where only unweighted scenarios are available, counting
target-achieving cases provides coverage information rather than an automatic probability estimate.
A worked comparison of learning and robust action
Suppose Maya is considering two specialized arrangements, A and B, and a robust arrangement R. This
abstract payoff model stipulates that all three meet their basic resource and authority requirements. The
operating target may be missed and is penalized in value; it is not yet a universal feasibility gate. The
next section examines what changes if target achievement is mandatory.
The arrangements already accommodate both modeled loading and transit outcomes, so only O affects
their payoffs. Establishing such coverage in a real operation would require its own evidence and feasible

When is the apparently better strategy inadmissible?
Suppose the operating target is a mandatory requirement: every modeled state must achieve it. Then A
and B are not adequate fixed plans. The noisy-study policy also fails that gate because an erroneous
report has positive probability. Its expected value of 90 cannot establish the required all-state support.

The study’s net improvement subtracts its cost in the same value units. Free optional information that
can be ignored does not reduce the optimum when it leaves the available choices unchanged. A real study
can consume time, money, and capacity, so it may change those choices as well as supply information.
This preserves the distinction between completion, feasibility, preference, and acceptance. A computed
expected value is a comparison under a model. It does not waive a mandatory condition or record a
human decision.
What do control and contingency preparation add?
An effective intervention can change what happens. A process change might make an operating condition
more predictable; a valid reservation might secure a resource. Such claims need support for their causal
effect, scope, duration, and failure modes. Recording a request or choosing a convenient value does not
establish that control was obtained.
An arrangement can also avoid a dependency. If a route does not need a consolidation loading window,
uncertainty about that window can cease to matter to its feasibility. The uncertain condition has not
thereby been learned or eliminated from the world.
A contingency policy specifies observable triggers and later actions. “If the window is lost, use the other
service” is only useful if the loss is observable early enough and that service can still be authorized, funded,
and booked. reassessment_trigger, contingency_response, and response_deadline should preserve those
dependencies. Examining vulnerabilities and improving performance across plausible futures is the
contribution of robust planning.[2]
For the soft-target teaching example, an actionable proposed learning rule is: “Obtain Y before the
commitment cutoff. If Y = 0, propose A; if Y = 1, propose B. The study costs 10 value units. If it
cannot finish while those choices remain available, reassess the study purchase and the current robust
action.” Human acceptance is still needed for the study and the resulting operating action, within their
respective authority.
Put the comparison beside the decision record
The plan_contents identifies whether we are buying evidence, intervening, selecting a fixed arrangement,
or preparing a contingent policy. assessed_conditions retains the probability and payoff assumptions,
the observation stage, and the scope of the operating checks. The established_finding reports what
those checks and calculations support. preferred_options and preference_basis preserve the accepted
comparison rule, including whether target failure is permitted at all.
next_action, acceptance_status, and decision_owner then identify the particular human commitment.
Unknown approval status can be investigated; obtaining new approval requires an actual authorized
decision. A forecast of acceptance is not the acceptance itself.

Decision Lab’s public shipment and gifting CLI/SDK guides preserve distinctions between completed
assessments, checked feasible plans, established preferences, and operating decisions. The gifting example,
for instance, makes an earlier external correction preferable only after a confirmation covers the current
insert version; the cheaper in-house correction continues to pass.[4] The information and utility calculations
in this article are a proposed teaching layer, not reported outputs of those existing examples.
A real extension would need reviewed outcome measures, probabilities where justified, feasible responses,
funding and timing checks, and retained human decisions. Its purpose would be to compare what a course
of action enables, rather than reward the largest reduction in a single uncertainty number.
Before the next commitment, finish this statement: “If we observe this result, we will propose this action;
otherwise, we will propose that response. We need the result by this time, it will cost this much, and this
person must accept the next action.” Those entries connect learning to a decision that can still change.
