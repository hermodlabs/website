# Gift-kit readiness

Three fictional stories follow Leah from a failed rehearsal to a supported correction, then show how a developer can continue a flagged campaign in the team's own tools. The commitment is 120 usable kits by the end of the day, within the recorded expense limit and operating requirements.

## Findings and operating events

An evidence assessment interprets supplied scoped diagnostic records under the declared check model. A commitment assessment compares complete correction packages, including timing, expense, resources, permissions and required booking confirmation. The planned ten-minute rehearsal exceeds the unchanged five-minute minimum. A deadline change preserves the original scope of permissions and confirmations.

The custom continuation retains separate choice, authorization, correction, passed rehearsal and handoff records. A later failed rehearsal can withdraw current completion support while earlier history remains available. Calculated support does not establish that correction or rehearsal occurred, authenticate records or perform the work.
