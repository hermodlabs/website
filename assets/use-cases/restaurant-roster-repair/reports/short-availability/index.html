Scope and retained basis

Elena is comparing four supplied complete repairs, not generating every possible roster. Monday–Thursday work is retained as individual worked-minute totals and latest shift ends; it is not retrospectively audited. The declared ten-hour rest and forty-hour weekly limits are authored assumptions, not a finding of legal compliance.

Fixed four-hour services in this fictional fixture have no in-shift breaks. Required breaks must be removed from working availability and covered explicitly. Actual availability, consent, qualifications, payroll premiums and publication require operating records or integrations. No staff notification or roster publication occurs.

The comparison and statuses above are rendered from the retained native SDK assessment. The renderer formats findings and does not implement scheduling rules.
