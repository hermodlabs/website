Cultivation Advisor / Grower Product Partner

Part-time contract role for someone with real cultivation or grow-operations experience who can help shape an environmental monitoring and mapping platform into something growers would actually use.

Part-time Contract Remote-friendly Startup / early product

About the Role

We’re building an environmental monitoring and mapping platform for cultivation, and we want someone with real-world experience to help us keep it grounded in operational reality.

This is not a coding role, not a hardware engineering role, and not a corporate management role. It is a practical feedback role for someone who understands how grows actually work and can tell us when something is useful, unrealistic, overbuilt, or genuinely valuable.

We want someone who can look at what we’re building and say: growers would care about this, growers would ignore this, this solves a real problem, or this is missing the part that actually matters operationally.

What the Platform Is Trying to Do

The platform is intended to touch, in some meaningful way, functions that overlap with several categories already familiar to the market. We are not claiming to be all of them at once in finished enterprise form. The goal is to demonstrate that a serious platform can be built with commodity hardware and open-source software while staying focused on real cultivation needs.

Controllers and dashboards Comparable in category to TrolMaster and Argus.
Sensor monitoring platforms Comparable in category to Pulse.
Crop steering tools Comparable in category to AROYA.
Automation platforms Comparable in category to Growlink.
Air sampling-oriented monitoring Comparable in category to Sporecyte.
Imaging and AI operational visibility Comparable in category to Taranis.

What You’d Actually Do

  • Review product ideas, concepts, mockups, and early features.
  • React to workflows and say whether they make sense in the real world.
  • Help us understand what operators actually care about.
  • Give blunt feedback on what is useful versus what is just technically interesting.
  • Help shape alerts, visibility tools, and product direction around real cultivation problems.
  • Act as a reality check as we build.

Who This Is For

  • You have real cultivation or grow-operations experience.
  • You understand environmental control, crop steering, irrigation, facility conditions, or day-to-day grow problems.
  • You can explain what actually matters to growers.
  • You’re comfortable giving direct feedback.
  • You like the idea of helping shape an early product before it gets polished and corporate.

You Do Not Need To

  • Write code.
  • Design hardware.
  • Understand every technical detail.
  • Pretend every idea is good.

Why This Role Exists

We are building an open-source project that is also meant to show a broader commercial direction. The idea is to demonstrate that a serious environmental monitoring and mapping platform does not have to be locked behind expensive closed systems.

That means we need input from someone who understands cultivation reality, not just technology.

What Good Looks Like

  • You can quickly spot what growers would care about.
  • You can separate operational value from technical novelty.
  • You can say when a workflow feels wrong in practice.
  • You make the product more grounded, not more abstract.

Nice If You Have It

  • Experience with commercial grow tech, controllers, dashboards, sensors, automation tools, or crop steering systems.
  • Experience helping other growers troubleshoot operational issues.
  • Strong instincts for the difference between “interesting” and “actually useful.”
