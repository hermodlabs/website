About the Role

We are a signal analysis and processing company building environmental modeling systems for controlled environments. A core part of our work is what we call the Real Time Actionability Framework: an attempt to reason about when localized environmental behavior becomes operationally meaningful quickly enough to support intervention.

Our view is that microclimates are governed by geometry + air forcing + buoyancy + timing. We are interested in representing those dynamics with tools such as Markov simulations and related stochastic approaches, while exploring where evolutionary algorithms and modern AI/ML methods can add useful leverage.

This is a consulting role for someone who can act as a true subject matter expert, strengthen the intellectual foundations of the framework, and help us connect modeling ideas to practical system design.

Open Source Project and Commercial Platform

We are also building an open-source project that serves as both a public reference implementation and a promotional piece for the company. It is meant to demonstrate that a credible environmental monitoring and mapping platform can be built using commodity hardware and open software.

The commercial platform builds on top of that foundation with more advanced sensing and inference, including audio tomography. This consultant role exists to help make sure the modeling and reasoning layer behind that commercial direction is equally serious.

What the Framework Is Trying to Capture

We are interested in modeling microclimates as evolving spatial-temporal behaviors rather than treating them as static averages. The aim is to better understand how room geometry, airflow, buoyancy-driven behavior, and operational timing interact to produce local transitions, persistence, and action-relevant conditions.

Geometry How room layout, structures, boundaries, and placement shape local conditions.
Air forcing How HVAC, fans, vents, returns, and operational forcing alter transport and mixing.
Buoyancy How heat and density gradients produce stratification, lift, settling, and directional flow.
Timing How transitions, delays, recurrence, and sequencing change what becomes operationally meaningful.
