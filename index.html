Help shape what comes next

Your work.
Our next pilots.

We’re seeking professionals, teams, and organizations to help shape Decision Lab. Explore the proposed scope and tell us about a real use case.

For professional practitioners and organizations

Professional

Bring automation into your decision work.

Use tools such as ChatGPT to find relevant information and prepare mappings into model code. You write and review the code; a programmable interface and connected sources automate the work around it.

Proposed pilot scope

  • Programmable CLI for scripted workflows
  • No character limits on model code
  • Connectors for email, Slack, and databases
  • MCP integration with compatible AI tools
  • LLM-assisted discovery and mapping of source information

For people working together

Team

Give each contribution a clear owner.

Bring evidence, reviews, and decisions into a shared record. Help shape a workflow where each reviewer records their own approval against the revision they examined, making responsibility and the basis for a decision clear.

Proposed pilot scope

  • Shared cases
  • Assigned reviews
  • Reviewer-authored approvals tied to specific revisions
  • A common history of contributions and decisions

For organizations

Enterprise

Coordinate decisions across teams and programs.

Explore consistent oversight when several groups depend on the same work. Help shape how decision records connect to your organization’s access policies, existing systems, and operating requirements.

Proposed pilot scope

  • Governance across teams and projects
  • Centralized identity and access management
  • Configurable retention policies
  • System integrations
  • Deployment options for controlled environments
  • Support commitments
