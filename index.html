03 / The approach

Make reasoning visible.
Keep judgment human.

Decision engineering makes the basis for a choice explicit enough to inspect, discuss, and revise. Strategic thinking is one part of that broader work.

01

Make assumptions inspectable.

See the conditions behind a finding. Distinguish what was observed, what was assumed, and what follows from the model.

Meaning before inference
02

Make improvement explicit.

Name whose outcomes matter, which criteria define improvement, and what tradeoffs remain. Keep those criteria open to challenge and the final judgment with people.

Criteria before conclusions
03

Keep conclusions revisable.

Change an assumption or replace an option. Trace which relationships are affected and what needs another look.

Consequences before judgment

An essential boundary

A consistent model can still be incomplete or wrong. Reasoning must remain open to better evidence, revised definitions, and human correction.
