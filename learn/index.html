The seven-part series

Follow a plan.
Understand its connections.

The series develops one inventory example before extending the approach to evidence, responsibility, and revision. It explains the shared foundation behind our tools, wherever the decision takes place.

01–05

Start with an inventory plan

The first five articles use the inventory example to examine what a plan needs, how its parts depend on one another, and what changes when a different option is chosen.

Build the reasoning one relationship at a time.

06

Connect evidence and responsibility

Extend the plan beyond its intended outcome. Consider what supports a claim, what still needs to be checked, and who is responsible for following through.

Make the work behind a conclusion visible.

07

Reconsider the plan when things change

Return to the relationships behind a decision when new information arrives. Identify which assumptions and conclusions need another look.

Keep the reasoning open to revision.
