Development

Build for the way
you work.

We turn your use case into Decision Lab models, custom applications, and reports. We work with your domain, users, information, and requirements to build tools that support the decisions you need to make.

Model the decision.

We define the choices, dependencies, evidence, and constraints that matter, then build a model that captures how they fit together.

Develop the application.

We write custom logic, connect the information your use case needs, and build an application around how people will use the model.

Build useful reports.

We create reports that help people compare options, understand consequences, and act on the results. The output is shaped around the questions your users need to answer.

We can start from a defined requirement or help you use evidence from testing to decide what to build next.

Test and learn consulting

Test the idea.
Learn what matters.

Our experts in testing and experimental design help you turn business goals into questions you can test. We work with you to define what you need to learn, choose meaningful measures of success, and design an experiment to address the question.

We help you interpret the results, assess what the evidence supports, and understand what remains uncertain. Use the learning to decide what to change, test again, or scale in support of your business goal.
