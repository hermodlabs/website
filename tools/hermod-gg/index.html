02 / The platform in play

Make the reasoning
part of the conversation.

A pick contributes to a plan through capabilities, conditions, and relationships with allies and opponents. Replacing that pick gives players a reason to reexamine the plan.

Hermod.gg applies our decision engineering platform to team strategy. A different pick is useful in relation to the team’s objectives, the capabilities it needs, and the tradeoffs players are willing to make. Players keep the judgment; the tool helps them examine the reasons.

Bring the same approach to a decision outside the game. Try Decision Lab for free to work through an individual review, examine its supporting evidence, and create a basic report. The Free personal version is in public beta.
