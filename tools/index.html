Choosing a team’s next pick and reviewing evidence for certification both involve objectives, conditions, alternatives, and a judgment about what the evidence supports.

Both applications make those relationships explicit. Their domain models and workflows differ; the platform provides a shared foundation for comparing choices and examining what would count as an improvement.
