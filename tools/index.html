03 / What connects them

Understand the choice.
See what depends on it.

Choosing a team’s next pick and deciding which test to run next both involve objectives, conditions, alternatives, and a judgment about what the evidence supports.

Both applications make those relationships explicit. Their domain models and workflows differ; the platform provides a shared foundation for comparing choices and examining what would count as an improvement.
