01 / Our first use case

CMMC certification.
Make the review inspectable.

Understand what a claim depends on, which evidence supports it, and what still needs a decision.

Our first use case supports the work of preparing for CMMC certification: connecting requirements to a system arrangement, examining the scope of supporting evidence, and identifying questions that need follow-up.

When the arrangement or evidence changes, revisit the affected claims and responsibilities. Review Lab supports human review; it does not issue certifications.

02 / The work behind a conclusion

Make the criteria clear.
Keep the questions visible.

A supportable decision connects the desired outcome, the evidence available, and the conditions that must hold.

01

Define what the review needs to establish.

Make the requirements, decision criteria, and responsibilities explicit. Clarify what would count as an improvement and whose work or outcomes it affects.

Know what the decision is for.

02

Inspect the supporting evidence.

Connect a claim to the observations that support it. Keep the scope, assumptions, and unanswered questions visible enough to examine and challenge.

See what supports the conclusion.

03

Reconsider what changes.

Compare alternatives and follow the effects of a change. Identify which claims, checks, or responsibilities need another look before relying on an earlier decision.

Keep conclusions open to revision.
