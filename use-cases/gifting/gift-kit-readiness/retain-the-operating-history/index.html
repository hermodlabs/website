Preserve the history behind the decision.

Keep a chosen correction and subsequent operating records connected to their basis, so later evidence changes what the team can claim without erasing what happened.

Jonathan Nacionales · 5 min read

The case in brief

At stake. A supported correction proposal does not establish that 120 usable kits were handed over. Leah needs the selected plan and subsequent source records to remain connected when the next person reopens the case.

How it resolves. The custom Handoff Desk retains the choice, authority, correction, rehearsal and handoff separately. The authored gate supports the original completion claim only while its recorded basis holds; a later failed rehearsal withdraws that support while preserving the earlier handoff and its history.

On this page
  1. Retain a specific choice, not just the preferred label.
  2. Give each operating assertion its own scope.
  3. Let a later contradiction change the supported claim.

Leah’s desk has calculated a workable response. Someone still has to choose the work, authorize its expense and carry it out. If those events live only in disconnected messages, the next coordinator cannot tell which records support the displayed completion claim.

The business account of the handoff explains what Leah needs to establish. The developer’s question here is narrower: how can the application preserve that basis when the case is saved, reopened or contradicted by a later record?

Retain a specific choice, not just the preferred label.

The Handoff Desk treats selection as an operating action separate from calculation and report inspection. Its sample choice helper resolves the native assessment and checks two public SDK predicates:

Assessment completeness and the existence of a feasible witness are separate claims. A completed assessment can find every package blocked. The helper also requires a current result and resolves one exact complete plan, including its candidate start, rather than arbitrarily selecting among tied or ambiguous alternatives.

The chosen plan retains its choices and evaluation hash. Later records can then refer to that specific basis. These guards coordinate SDK findings; the sample’s helper is not an SDK authorization service.

Give each operating assertion its own scope.

For the urgent GIFT-321 case, the chosen $400 external response is followed by separate sourced records. The application preserves their references, event and recording times, applicable configuration and links:

The authored gate checks these relationships against the original promise.
RecordRelationship the application retains
Spending authorizationThe original campaign, selected response and $400 scope.
Actual correction120 corrected kits, the original input configuration and the resulting insert-5, destination-3 and checkout-2 output.
Actual rehearsalA passed ten-minute test on that corrected output, linked to its correction record.
Actual handoffAll 120 kits, that corrected scope and passed rehearsal, recorded at minute 1,435 before the original 1,440 cutoff.

The SDK retains the native case and history. The domain-authored workflow supplies the rules for scope, quantity, chronology, latest records and original completion. Company code records the external assertions and connects its operating systems. These rules are not invented by the shared report component.

The gate now supports “Original handoff recorded complete.” It has assessed supplied assertions, not authenticated their sources or performed the work.

Let a later contradiction change the supported claim.

A rehearsal failure on the same corrected output is recorded at minute 1,436. Under this authored gate, the later complete rehearsal record supersedes the earlier positive result. Support for the original completion claim is withdrawn; the earlier physical handoff and its source record remain in history.

This is a feedback loop with an explicit basis. The application does not search backward for a favorable assertion or quietly move the deadline. Leah sees why she needs to investigate. A further correction would require fresh, appropriately linked evidence.

Save the native case, then compare compatible findings.

The desk serializes the retained workflow through the SDK:

The sample’s local storage adapter uses an expected-hash guard against silently overwriting another revision. Native serialization and the company’s storage implementation have separate responsibilities. Reopening the file preserves the original promise, selected-plan basis and later negative record alongside the earlier positive history.

For compatible planning calculations, public lab.reports.compareFindings provides structured differences. That comparison does not authenticate operating events or decide whether a rehearsal establishes completion; the authored domain gate answers that question. A changed review time also makes affected calculations stale rather than rewriting their earlier basis.

The useful result is a continuing account the next person can inspect: what was proposed, what was chosen, what was recorded and what the latest evidence supports. Leah can respond to the failed test without losing the history behind the earlier decision. The full Handoff Desk source includes the operating helpers and storage integration around these SDK operations.

Sources and scope

Implementation and captures

The Gift-kit Preflight CLI and Gift-kit Handoff Desk repositories contain the related public examples. The retained implementation preserves the exact source behind these excerpts; its source manifest records file identities. The cost-first app remains version 3; this calculation workflow is version 4.1.

The screenshots show the user-verified local production preview. They do not claim that the updated app has been published. The capture and operating verification retains the six campaign outcomes, CLI-to-browser import, native exports, inspection checks, completion and later failed rehearsal. Exact built asset identities and source-file identities are recorded separately. Historical capture receipts remain intact.

Worked-example boundaries

Leah, Jordan, campaigns, quotes, diagnostic reports and operating assertions are fictional. Each campaign has independently allocated resources. The finite model checks authored fault combinations, packages and candidate starts; it does not infer probabilities or discover unlisted recoveries. The planned ten-minute rehearsal is distinct from the five-minute model minimum and an actual passed test.

The company owns trustworthy sources, input mappings, identity, storage and external integrations. Native case history preserves assertions and their calculation basis; it does not authenticate records, authorize spending or perform work. Shared-resource planning and partial-delivery aggregation need additional modeling.
