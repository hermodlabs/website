Sources and scope

Nina, the release, estimates, resource records, fees and customer discussions are fictional. The published release workflow evaluates four authored complete arrangements against the recorded operating basis. It establishes feasibility under declared requirements and deliberately does not rank arrangements. The working window represents an exclusive qualified allocation that may span shifts, not one person working a 16-hour shift. The model retains the original promise when scope or date is revised. Calculations do not execute tests, obtain customer agreement, certify software quality, estimate delivery probability or publish the release. Jira imports provide only the two mapped workload totals; other operating facts need separate records.
