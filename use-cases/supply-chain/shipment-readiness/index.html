The business situation

A launch to protect. Several needs to balance.

Maya has orders to fulfill and receiving windows to meet. The launch team wants earlier arrivals, finance wants to control spending, and operations needs the readiness records and permissions to support each plan. New quotes, tighter limits, revised orders and additional requirements change the options she can recommend.
